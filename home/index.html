Booking to class

What happens after you request?

Here is the booking sequence, including when payment happens and how the class is created.

01

Request an open time

Choose an available slot from a teacher’s schedule. The request stays pending until the teacher responds.

02

The teacher approves or declines

Your booking status updates when the teacher responds, so you know whether the time is confirmed.

03

Pay after approval

Once approved, complete payment in the student portal. Confirmed payment creates the class session.
